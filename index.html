Lipa Namba

One Lipa Namba. That's the whole idea.

Every merchant and mobile money agent on PointPesa gets a single Lipa Namba — the same kind of number you already use for mobile money and bank transfers. Customers and clients dial it, scan it, or pick it from any network's or bank's menu — for a sale, a cash-in, or a cash-out — and it works no matter where the money is coming from. You stop juggling four or five different numbers at the counter.

Serving five networks shouldn't mean five different numbers.

This isn't just a shop problem. Retailers, service businesses, and mobile money agents handling cash-in and cash-out all day hit the same wall, several times an hour:

A different Lipa Namba for every network

One for M-Pesa, one for Airtel, one for a bank QR, one for Mixx. Customers scan the wrong one, agents recite the wrong number under pressure, and cash-in/cash-out clients get confused about which one applies to them.

No single record of who paid what

Sales, cash-ins, and cash-outs land in four or five separate apps, so closing out a day's books means checking that many phones and screenshots to add it all up.

Support lives on hold, not on WhatsApp

When a payment fails — whether it's a sale, a cash-in, or a cash-out — you're stuck calling a hotline instead of getting an answer where you already spend your day.
