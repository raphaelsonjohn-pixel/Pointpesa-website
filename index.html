Lipa Namba

One Lipa Namba. That's the whole idea.

Every merchant and mobile money agent on Pointpesa gets a single Lipa Namba, the same kind of number you already use for mobile money and bank transfers. Customers and clients can dial it, scan it, or select it from any network's or bank's menu for a sale, a cash-in, or a cash-out, and it works no matter where the money is coming from. You stop juggling four or five different numbers at the counter.

Serving five networks shouldn't mean five different numbers.

This isn't only a shop problem. Retailers, service businesses, and mobile money agents who handle cash-in and cash-out all day run into the same friction, often several times an hour.

A different Lipa Namba for every network

One number for M-Pesa, another for Airtel, another for a bank QR, and another for Mixx. Customers scan the wrong one, agents recite the wrong number under pressure, and cash-in and cash-out clients aren't always sure which number applies to them.

No single record of who paid what

Sales, cash-ins, and cash-outs land in four or five separate apps. Closing out the day's books means checking that many phones and screenshots just to add it all up.

Support lives on hold, not on WhatsApp

When a payment fails, whether it's a sale, a cash-in, or a cash-out, you're stuck calling a hotline instead of getting an answer where you already spend your day.
